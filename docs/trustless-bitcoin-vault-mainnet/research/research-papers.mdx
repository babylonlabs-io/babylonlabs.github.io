The formal papers behind TBV. They document the protocol design and the cryptographic construction that lets Bitcoin enforce the vault.

## A Bitcoin-Charged Crypto Economy with Trustless Vaults

The TBV protocol paper. It introduces the Trustless Vault: a way to lock native BTC and use it as collateral in on-chain applications without handing custody to a bridge or a custodian. It covers the vault lifecycle (peg-in, activation, redemption), the pre-signed Bitcoin transaction graph and self-recovery paths that make the vault enforceable on Bitcoin itself, and how vaults plug into applications such as the Aave v4 integration.

## BABE: Verifying Proofs on Bitcoin Made 1000x Cheaper

The BABE paper. BABE is the cryptographic construction that makes verifying succinct proofs on Bitcoin dramatically cheaper — on the order of 1000x — combining witness encryption with garbled Groth16 verification, integrated with Winternitz one-time signatures (WOTS) and a cut-and-choose setup that tolerates a malicious setup. It underpins the challenge and verification flow that keeps the protocol trustless.
