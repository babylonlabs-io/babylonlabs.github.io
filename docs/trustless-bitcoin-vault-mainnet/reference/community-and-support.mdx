title: Community & support
---

Where to get help, ask questions, and reach the team. For anything that is **not** a security vulnerability, use the community and support channels below. Security issues follow a separate, private path — see **Responsible disclosure**.

## Official channels

The current list of official TBV community and support channels is published on the official Babylon / TBV website and documentation. Non-security questions — using the protocol, integrating an application, operator onboarding, general help — belong there.

| Channel | Use it for |
|---|---|
| Official website & documentation | Canonical links, announcements, and the authoritative index of every other channel |
| Community chat / Discord | General questions, discussion, and community support |
| X (Twitter) | Official announcements and updates |
| GitHub | Public repositories, where applicable |

Specific channel handles and invite links are **to be announced at launch** and are always published from the official website first.

## Avoid impersonators and scam links

Only trust links published on the official Babylon / TBV website and documentation. Impersonation accounts, fake "support" direct messages, and links shared by strangers are common in this space:

- The team will **never** DM you first asking for funds, seed phrases, private keys, or your WOTS keypair.
- Do not click invite or "support" links forwarded by unknown parties — verify every channel against the official site.
- When in doubt, navigate to the official website directly rather than following a link someone sent you.

## Security issues go elsewhere

Do **not** post suspected vulnerabilities in community channels. Report them privately through **Responsible disclosure**, and do not disclose publicly before a fix is deployed. Rewards for valid reports are handled through the **Bug bounty** program.
