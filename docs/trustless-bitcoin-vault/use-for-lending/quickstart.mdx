# Quickstart

> **Network and release check:** Use [Mainnet setup](../network-info/mainnet/setup.mdx) for Bitcoin Mainnet and Ethereum Mainnet. Use [Testnet setup](../network-info/testnet/setup.mdx) for Signet and Sepolia. Screenshots show the earlier testnet UI, not a confirmed mainnet release. Check the selected network's wallets, assets, caps, fees, and timing before signing.

This walkthrough covers the Aave v4 integration from vault creation through borrowing, repayment, and Bitcoin redemption.

Connect both wallets, create and activate a vault, borrow, repay, and redeem. Confirmation times and challenge windows depend on the selected deployment. Each step links to its detailed guide.

A BTCVault locks BTC on the Bitcoin Network and makes it usable as collateral on Ethereum without bridges or wrapping. The TBV app drives a full peg-in, borrow, repay, and peg-out cycle end to end.

The public testnet runs on signet, a Bitcoin test network. The BTC and stablecoins involved have no monetary value. Some flows are still under active development; report issues to the team via the channels listed in [Community & support](../reference/community-and-support.mdx).

:::info Public testnet limits
Each address and each position is capped on the Aave application. See [Protocol parameters](../network-info/testnet/protocol-parameters.mdx) for the live numeric limits.
:::

## Before you start

Choose the network before connecting either wallet.

| Network | Bitcoin | Ethereum | Setup |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mainnet | Bitcoin Mainnet, real BTC | Ethereum Mainnet, real ETH | [Mainnet wallet setup](../network-info/mainnet/setup.mdx) |
| Testnet | Signet, test BTC | Sepolia, test ETH | [Testnet setup](../network-info/testnet/setup.mdx) |

You need a supported wallet pair, BTC for the deposit and Bitcoin fees, and ETH for Ethereum gas and protocol fees.

Read [Governance and committees](../technical-details/governance-and-committees.mdx) before using mainnet. For a code-based path, use [Borrow through the SDK](../building-on-tbv/borrow-with-sdk.mdx).

## Step 1: Open the TBV app

Open the official app link for the network selected above. Stop if the app and either wallet show different networks.

![App landing page in the pre-wallet state](/img/trustless-bitcoin-vault/confluence/use-for-lending/quickstart/image-20260527-084545.png)

## Step 2: Connect wallets

Click the **Connect** button at the top of the app or on any card.

Important: the Bitcoin wallet must be set to **Taproot (P2TR)** address type before connecting. Different address types can have different balances. Changing the address type does not move BTC. Use a faucet only on testnet.

The wallet picker then opens. Select a Bitcoin wallet using a Taproot address type and an Ethereum wallet to sign the on-chain calls.

![Wallet picker](/img/trustless-bitcoin-vault/confluence/use-for-lending/quickstart/image-20260527-084735.png)

## Step 3: Create a vault (peg-in)

A peg-in moves BTC into a vault on Bitcoin and registers it on Ethereum. The BTC stays on Bitcoin throughout, locked in a Taproot output. Nothing is bridged or wrapped.

From the **Collateral** section, click **Deposit**. Then:

**1. Enter the amount in BTC** within the selected network's caps. See [Mainnet parameters](../network-info/mainnet/protocol-parameters-and-caps.mdx) or [Testnet parameters](../network-info/testnet/protocol-parameters.mdx).

![Peg-in deposit amount entry](/img/trustless-bitcoin-vault/confluence/use-for-lending/quickstart/image-20260527-091245.png)

**2. Select a Vault Provider from the dropdown.**

**3. Confirm the Bitcoin and Ethereum transactions.**

![Confirm Bitcoin + Ethereum transactions](/img/trustless-bitcoin-vault/confluence/use-for-lending/quickstart/image-20260527-091308.png)

The app then runs the off-chain setup automatically: authentication, signature collection, acknowledgments. The full walkthrough is on [Create a vault](./create-a-vault.mdx).

The app recommends splitting your deposit into two vaults: a sacrificial vault sized to cover the protocol's expected seizure amount (placed first in liquidation order), and a protected vault holding the rest (placed second). The recommended split depends on the current deployment parameters. For the order-change UI, see [Reorder BTC vaults](./create-a-vault.mdx#reorder-vaults-later).

Wait for **Verified** status and the configured activation delay. Keep the recovery artifacts before activation. Review the **Activate** request in your wallet before its deadline. After confirmation, check that the vault supplies collateral to your position.

![Active vault in the Collateral section](/img/trustless-bitcoin-vault/use-for-lending/quickstart-06-activate-prompt.png)

## Step 4: Borrow stablecoins

Your vault is already supplied as collateral on the integrated Aave v4 market — a collateral record was created for it automatically when you activated. From this step on, you are interacting with the Aave v4 application layer; the underlying vault on Bitcoin sits unchanged.

Open the **Loans** section and click **Borrow**:

**1. Select an asset enabled on the chosen deployment.** The screenshots do not define the mainnet asset list.

![Borrow modal — select asset](/img/trustless-bitcoin-vault/confluence/use-for-lending/quickstart/screenshot-092-20260601-043442.png)

**2. Enter the amount and sign the borrow transaction.**

![Borrow modal — enter amount](/img/trustless-bitcoin-vault/confluence/use-for-lending/quickstart/screenshot-093-20260601-043515.png)

**3. Confirm the Ethereum transaction.**

![Borrow MetaMask transaction request](/img/trustless-bitcoin-vault/confluence/use-for-lending/quickstart/screenshot-097-20260601-043549.png)

**4. Wait for the portal to refresh the position preview and health factor.**

![Borrow confirmed](/img/trustless-bitcoin-vault/confluence/use-for-lending/quickstart/screenshot-100-20260601-043705.png)

![Loan visible in the Loans section](/img/trustless-bitcoin-vault/use-for-lending/quickstart-11-borrow-confirm.png)

The borrowed asset arrives in your Ethereum wallet. The app now shows your **health factor**, a single number summarizing how close the position is to liquidation. A higher number is safer.

## Step 5: Repay the debt

To close the position, repay what you borrowed.

From the **Loans** section, click **Repay**:

* **Repay full** closes the entire borrow and unlocks redemption.
* **Repay partial** pays down a portion; the position stays open with reduced debt.

![Repay form](/img/trustless-bitcoin-vault/confluence/use-for-lending/quickstart/screenshot-101-20260601-043734.png)

:::info Two MetaMask prompts
Repaying usually shows an ERC-20 spending cap approval first, then the repayment transaction. Approve a small buffer because interest can accrue while the transaction is pending; any excess stays in your wallet.
:::

![Repay MetaMask transaction request](/img/trustless-bitcoin-vault/confluence/use-for-lending/quickstart/screenshot-102-20260601-043800.png)

![Repay confirmed](/img/trustless-bitcoin-vault/confluence/use-for-lending/quickstart/screenshot-105-20260601-043835.png)

Interest accrues continuously until repayment, so the exact amount due is slightly higher than the principal borrowed. After full repayment, the vault is eligible for redemption.

## Step 6: Withdraw and redeem

Redeeming releases the underlying BTC from the vault back to your Bitcoin wallet. This step crosses back from the Aave v4 application layer into the TBV protocol layer.

From the **Collateral** section, select an **in use** vault and click **Withdraw**.

![Withdraw form — selected vault](/img/trustless-bitcoin-vault/confluence/use-for-lending/quickstart/30-withdraw-selected-vault.jpg)

Review the withdrawal amount, network fee rate, VP commission, and the nominated Bitcoin address.

![Withdraw review screen](/img/trustless-bitcoin-vault/confluence/use-for-lending/quickstart/31-withdraw-review.jpg)

Confirm the Ethereum transaction.

![Withdraw MetaMask transaction request](/img/trustless-bitcoin-vault/confluence/use-for-lending/quickstart/32-withdraw-metamask-transaction-request.jpg)

The redemption then runs in three protocol-side phases:

1. **Claim.** The Vault Provider generates a zero-knowledge proof in the background showing the debt is repaid, then submits a redemption claim on Bitcoin. The app shows progress.
2. **Challenge period.** The configured challenge window opens during which an App Keeper or Universal Challenger may dispute the claim. If no valid challenge appears, the claim finalizes.
3. **Payout.** Your BTC arrives at the address recorded in the vault.

![Withdraw processing](/img/trustless-bitcoin-vault/confluence/use-for-lending/quickstart/33-withdraw-processing.jpg)

![Withdraw initiated](/img/trustless-bitcoin-vault/confluence/use-for-lending/quickstart/34-withdraw-initiated.jpg)

![Withdraw pending on dashboard](/img/trustless-bitcoin-vault/confluence/use-for-lending/quickstart/35-withdraw-pending-dashboard.jpg)

The challenge window is part of the proof-verification mechanism. Use the selected network's parameters for its duration.

## If something stalls

Common scenarios and where to look:

* **Pre-PegIn still pending after a few hours:** check the Bitcoin block explorer (see [Setup](../network-info/testnet/setup.mdx)). If the transaction is propagated but not confirmed, wait — signet block times can be irregular.
* **Vault setup exceeds its deadline:** check the vault's configured acknowledgement and activation windows. A missed Ethereum deadline does not make the Bitcoin refund immediately spendable. Follow the refund timelock in [Create a vault](./create-a-vault.mdx).
* **Wallet won't connect or transaction rejected:** verify the wallet is on the correct network (see [Setup](../network-info/testnet/setup.mdx) for chain IDs).
* **Vault Provider not responding during redemption:** the depositor self-claim path applies. See [Withdraw & redeem](./withdraw-and-redeem.mdx).

For anything else, ask in the public-testnet support channel listed on [Community & support](../reference/community-and-support.mdx).

## You've completed a full cycle

The cycle ends when the Bitcoin payout confirms. Keep the vault ID and transaction receipts for your records.
