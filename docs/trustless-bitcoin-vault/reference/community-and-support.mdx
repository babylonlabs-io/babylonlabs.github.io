# Community & support

Where to ask questions, follow announcements, file issues, or reach the team about Trustless Bitcoin Vault (TBV), on either network.

Security vulnerabilities follow a separate, private path — see [Responsible disclosure](/trustless-bitcoin-vault/security/responsible-disclosure/). Do **not** post them in community channels.

## Where to ask what

* **Stuck mid-flow on the TBV Testnet app:** Discord, in the Testnet support channel
* **Found a bug in the UI or contracts (non-security):** open an issue on GitHub
* **Question about how something works conceptually:** start with [Quickstart](/trustless-bitcoin-vault/use-for-lending/quickstart/) and [TBV vs alternatives](/trustless-bitcoin-vault/start-here/tbv-vs-alternatives/), then ask in Discord
* **Lost your WOTS keypair and claimer artifacts:** if the Vault Provider for that vault is also unresponsive, contact the team to initiate Security Council recovery. See [Withdraw & redeem](/trustless-bitcoin-vault/use-for-lending/withdraw-and-redeem/) for the self-claim path's input requirements
* **Press, partnerships, or business inquiries:** email the address above

## Response time expectations

Team response times vary by time zone; expect within 24 hours on business days for non-urgent questions in Discord. For time-sensitive issues during a Testnet flow (e.g. peg-in stuck for many hours), use the dedicated Testnet support channel on Discord and the team can usually respond faster.

## Safety reminders

Only trust links published on the official Babylon / TBV website and documentation. Impersonation accounts, fake "support" direct messages, and links shared by strangers are common in this space:

* The team will **never** ask for a private key, seed phrase, signed transaction, or your WOTS keypair — in any channel. Anyone making such a request is impersonating the team.
* The team will **never** DM you first asking for funds, transactions, or credentials. If you receive a suspicious DM, report it in the public channel.
* Do not click invite or "support" links forwarded by unknown parties — verify every channel against the official site.
* When in doubt, navigate to the official website directly rather than following a link someone sent you.

## Security issues go elsewhere

Do **not** post suspected vulnerabilities in community channels, and do not disclose publicly before a fix is deployed. Report them privately through [Responsible disclosure](/trustless-bitcoin-vault/security/responsible-disclosure/). Rewards for valid reports are handled through the [Bug bounty](/trustless-bitcoin-vault/security/bug-bounty/) program.
